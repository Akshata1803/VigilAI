How Vigil Works

The 9-engine analysis pipeline, explained

🧬

DOM Analyzer

Parses raw HTML to detect 20 structural dark patterns: pre-checked consent boxes, popup traps, drip pricing, shadow subscriptions, fake countdowns, bait-and-switch, and more.

🧠

NLP Engine

14 text categories, 200+ regex patterns. Catches confirmshaming, FUD marketing, trick questions, basket sneaking, COPPA risk, and fine print abuse.

🎨

Visual Analyzer

CSS color psychology, button misdirection via styling, urgency color abuse, contrast manipulation against WCAG 2.1 AA.

🛡️

Elite Privacy

15 categories: tracking pixels, session replay tools (Hotjar, FullStory, Clarity), browser fingerprinting, data broker integrations, geo-discrimination.

🍪

Consent Auditor

10 GDPR consent checks: accept-only banners, deceptive button hierarchy, cookie walls, consent bundling, pre-ticked boxes, third-party pre-activation.

🔗

Link & Journey

Click-path analysis: cancel vs sign-up asymmetry, anchor mismatches, legal link burial, dark download funnels, unsubscribe obstruction.

📖

Readability Engine

Actual Flesch-Kincaid grade calculation, jargon density scoring (per 1000 words), contradictory statement detection, and forced arbitration discovery.

🧩

Behavioural AI Exclusive

6 compound dark funnels, Cialdini 6-principle mapping, industry risk multipliers (gambling/children/crypto), regulatory enforcement probability score (0–100).

⚖️

Legal Framework Engine

Every finding cites a specific regulation: GDPR Art. 7, FTC ROSCA Act, COPPA, CCPA, WCAG 2.1, EU DSA, ePrivacy Directive, CAN-SPAM, ASA CAP Code.
